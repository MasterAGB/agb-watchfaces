Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short: our watch faces do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything they show stays on your watch.

Who we are

This policy covers the watch faces published on Google Play by AGB, an independent developer based in Latvia: Onyx Guilloché, Royal Sapphire, Bordeaux Ruby, Diamond Noir, Emerald Deco, and any future AGB watch faces. Contact: BoarRevenger@gmail.com.

What the watch faces show

The watch faces are built with Google's Watch Face Format, which means they are designs only and contain no program code. Your watch's own system provides the values they display:

  • time and date;
  • step count and daily step goal;
  • heart rate;
  • battery level;
  • weather conditions and temperature;
  • the tilt of your wrist from the watch's motion sensor, used only to move the light effects.

These values are drawn on your watch's screen and nowhere else. The watch faces cannot save, copy or send them, and we never receive them.

What we do not do

  • No internet access - weather comes from your watch's own weather service.
  • No Android permissions requested.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts or sign-in.

Purchases

Purchases, payments and refunds are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details. We receive only the sales information Google Play provides to developers (such as order number, date and country), which we use for accounting and to handle refunds.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

The watch faces are not directed at children under 13, and they collect no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from using the watch faces, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. For any privacy question, email BoarRevenger@gmail.com. You can also contact the Latvian data protection authority, the Data State Inspectorate.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
