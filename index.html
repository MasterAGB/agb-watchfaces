On your wrist

Light that follows your wrist

Gems and polished metal catch the light as you turn your wrist. Every stone has facets that flash at their own angles, and a soft band of light travels across the bezel.

Twinkling stones

A few stones sparkle on their own, slowly, even when you hold still. You can switch this off in the watch face settings.

Everything you need, in jewellery

Date, heart rate, steps with a ring that fills toward your daily goal, live weather with an icon, and battery level - each in its own gem-set dial.

Calm always-on display

When your wrist is down, a dim, static version shows the time and date using less than a tenth of the screen, to save battery.

Compatibility

Round Wear OS watches with Wear OS 5.1 or newer. Designed and tested on the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2. Tap shortcuts for steps and weather open Samsung Health and Samsung Weather on Galaxy watches.
