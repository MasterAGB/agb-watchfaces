Install a watch face

  1. Open the watch face's page on Google Play on your phone and choose your watch as the install target.
  2. On the watch, touch and hold the current watch face.
  3. Swipe to the end and tap + Add watch face.
  4. Find the face under Downloaded and tap it.

Questions

Heart rate shows "--"

Your watch hasn't measured your heart rate yet. Wear the watch snugly, or measure once in Samsung Health; the value appears after the next reading.

Weather shows "--"

The watch has no weather data yet. Open the Weather app on your watch once, and make sure your phone is connected and location is allowed for weather.

Steps show "12k"

Above 9,999 steps the count is shortened so it fits the dial: 12k means about twelve thousand.

The light effects don't move

They follow your wrist while the screen is on. The always-on display is static on purpose, to save battery.

How do I turn off the twinkling?

Touch and hold the watch face, tap Customise, and switch off Twinkle.

The battery ring turned red

That's a reminder: the battery is at 15% or less.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund from your Google Play order history.

Compatibility

Round Wear OS watches with Wear OS 5.1 or newer. Designed and tested on the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2. Tap shortcuts for steps and weather open Samsung Health and Samsung Weather on Galaxy watches.
