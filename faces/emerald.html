Emerald Deco

An emerald-green Art Deco sunburst dial with a stepped ziggurat motif, a sculpted silver bezel set with four emeralds, diamond baguette indices and bold gold deco numerals.

Coming soon to Google Play

Features

  • Light that follows your wrist. Gems and polished metal catch the light as you turn your wrist. Every stone has facets that flash at their own angles, and a soft band of light travels across the bezel.
  • Twinkling stones. A few stones sparkle on their own, slowly, even when you hold still. You can switch this off in the watch face settings.
  • Everything you need, in jewellery. Date, heart rate, steps with a ring that fills toward your daily goal, live weather with an icon, and battery level - each in its own gem-set dial.
  • Calm always-on display. When your wrist is down, a dim, static version shows the time and date using less than a tenth of the screen, to save battery.

Always-on display

When your wrist is down, the face switches to a calm, dim version with the hands, numerals and date, and no animation - easy on the battery.

Compatibility

Round Wear OS watches with Wear OS 5.1 or newer. Designed and tested on the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2. Tap shortcuts for steps and weather open Samsung Health and Samsung Weather on Galaxy watches.

How to install

  1. Open the watch face's page on Google Play on your phone and choose your watch as the install target.
  2. On the watch, touch and hold the current watch face.
  3. Swipe to the end and tap + Add watch face.
  4. Find the face under Downloaded and tap it.
